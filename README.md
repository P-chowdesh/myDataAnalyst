 #  Customer Segmentation Analysis
 #  Project Overview

This project performs customer segmentation using data analysis techniques to identify different customer groups based on their purchasing behavior. The insights can help businesses improve marketing strategies and customer retention.<br>
This project focuses on customer segmentation by analyzing customer demographics and purchasing behavior. Using data analysis and clustering techniques, customers are grouped into distinct segments based on <br> factors such as annual income and spending patterns. The objective is to help businesses better understand their customers, improve targeted marketing campaigns, and enhance customer retention strategies.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation, visualization, and actionable business insights derived from the identified customer groups.<br>

#  DATASET VARIABLES:-

 invoice_no: <br>
A combination of the letter 'I' and a 6-digit integer uniquely assigned to each operation.<br>
customer_id: <br> 
A combination of the letter 'C' and a 6-digit integer uniquely assigned to each operation.<br>
gender: <br>
String variable of the customer's gender.<br>
age:  <br>
Positive Integer variable of the customers age.<br>
 category:<br>
String variable of the category of the purchased product.<br>
quantity: <br>
The quantities of each product (item) per transaction.<br>
price: <br>
Product price per unit in Turkish Liras (TL).<br>
payment_method: <br>
String variable of the payment method (cash, credit card or debit card) used for the transaction.<br>
invoice_date: <br>
The day when a transaction was generated.<br>
shopping_mall: <br>
String variable of the name of the shopping mall where the transaction was made.<br>

# Dataset Overview
This dataset contains shopping information from 10 different shopping malls between 2021 and 2023. We have gathered data from various age groups and genders to provide a comprehensive view of shopping habits<br> Istanbul. The dataset includes essential information such as invoice numbers, customer IDs, age, gender, payment methods, product categories, quantity, price, order dates, and shopping mall locations.<br>

The dataset consists of customer transaction records including demographic information, product category, purchase quantity, and payment details. It helps in analyzing revenue patterns, customer segments, and overall business performance.

## Insights:- 
Revenue Performance:-
Customer Segmentation Insights:-
      Female customers contribute approximately 60% of total transactions, making them the dominant segment.<br>
      Working-age customers (21–44 years) generate the highest purchase activity.<br>
      Adults and middle-aged customers drive overall business performance.<br>
Category Insights:-
      Clothing is the highest-performing category in transaction volume.<br>
      Cosmetics and Food & Beverage show strong mid-level demand.<br>
      Books and Technology have lower transaction frequency.<br>
      Customer demand is concentrated in fashion and lifestyle segments.<br>
Payment Method Insights:-
      Cash (45%) is the most preferred payment method.<br>
      Credit Card usage is strong (35%), showing purchasing power.<br>
      Digital payments (Credit + Debit) exceed 50%, indicating growing digital adoption.<br>
# Business Recommendations (suggestions):-
 Focus on High-Value Segments:-
        Target female and working-age customers with personalized campaigns.<br>
 Strengthen Top Categories:-
                    Expand Clothing inventory and introduce bundle offers.<br>
Improve Low-Performing Categories:-
                    Promote Books and Technology through discounts or combo deals.<br>
 Encourage Digital Payments:-
                    Offer cashback or rewards for card payments.<br>
                     Reduce cash handling operational cost.<br>
Increase AOV:-
                    Introduce product bundling and cross-selling strategies.<br>

## Conclusion:-
In conclusion, the analysis reveals strong revenue performance driven primarily by female and working-age customers. Clothing emerges as the dominant category, and although cash remains the most preferred<br>
payment method, digital adoption is significant. By focusing on high-performing segments and optimizing underperforming categories, the business can further enhance profitability and customer engagement.<br>
